# Royal Elegance design system

The current redesign keeps the Enhanced Classic Royal palette and replaces the split hero, tiny supporting typography and wizard form.

- Royal ivory #F8F4EC; champagne #E8DCC8; pearl #FFFDF8; antique gold #C4A46A; espresso #574737; muted rose #CBA9A3. Essential light-surface text is espresso; photographic hero text is pearl over a graduated warm overlay. Gold is decorative or appears against dark surfaces.
- Locally bundled Great Vibes: couple names on the revealed card, hero, formal invitation and closing. The closed envelope has an unlettered front and a centered wax seal. Names remain large on mobile. Cinzel: main ceremonial headings, dates and navigation. Cormorant Garamond: body, fields and readable supporting copy.
- Body copy 18–23px, form labels 18px, controls 18px, supporting text generally 14–17px, buttons/navigation 14–16px. Main headings use responsive 29–46px Cinzel; selected romantic display messages and names use larger script. Compact stationery details in the envelope use 12–18px according to space.
- The hero is edge-to-edge photography with a thin gold frame, a portrait source for phones and landscape source for desktop, names below the faces, and an integrated countdown. There is no side image card or disconnected countdown section.
- The formal double border and botanical stationery connect to the opening card. Two different new photos form the couple collage. The four-image editorial gallery keeps natural orientations and uses deliberate row offsets on desktop, a single column on phones, and a large accessible lightbox.
- One continuous RSVP surface uses clear labels, native radios, warm gold selection states, immediate nested reveals, inline validation, and compact inline confirmations.
- Twelve CSS hearts in fixed edge lanes drift slowly; eight remain visible on small phones. A separate envelope layer is needed because native dialogs occupy the browser top layer. Envelope hearts are 1.6 times the page heart size. Pointer events are disabled. Reduced motion hides all hearts.
- Section padding is 104px desktop / 72px phone; content widths are 1040–1120px, formal paper 738px, RSVP 750px. Navigation overlays the photograph and becomes an ivory menu on smaller displays. Touch controls are at least 44px.

Photograph usage and exact original filenames: `ASSET_INVENTORY.md`. No fabricated relationship narrative, cultural rituals, programme or audio is introduced.
